# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the maintainers at [Azerosoft](https://azerosoft.com) and everyone who takes part in this project, want `flutter_full_restart` to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as the issue tracker, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **hello@azerosoft.com**. Every report is reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful.
